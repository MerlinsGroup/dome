02 / Outcomes & measurement

What success looks like.

Success is assessed through consistent publishing, relevant audience growth, useful engagement and measurable registrations or bookings.

Initial benchmarks set after channel and tracking review.
12–16 / month
Core feed publications
Reels, TikToks, statics and carousels; platform versions where appropriate.
4–5 days / week
Stories across platforms
Instagram polls and votes, plus regular TikTok Story tests.
500 → 1,000
TikTok follower milestones
Starting from fewer than 500 followers; review progress monthly.

Leading indicators

  • Reach and watch-through among nearby audiences.
  • Profile visits, follower growth, saves, shares and comments.
  • Poll responses, Story views, link taps and enquiries.
  • Performance by format, hook and platform.

Commercial outcomes

  • Registrations and cost per registration.
  • Completed bookings and acquisition cost when measurable.
  • Attributed booking value and repeat visits where supported.
  • Weekly progress against agreed baseline measures.

Publishing quantities are proposed monthly outputs. Platform growth and commercial results are targets to manage towards, subject to approved content, tracking access and operational readiness.

03 / Visual identity & production

A consistent creative standard.

The same visual identity should be recognisable across Reels, TikToks, Stories, static posts, carousels and adverts. Editing should feel polished and natural.

Official identity only

Use approved logo files, fonts and colours. Never redraw or alter the mark.

Purposeful motion

Use premium animated typography, clean transitions and mobile-safe layouts.

Controlled grading

Maintain realistic colour, natural skin tones and controlled saturation.

Audio that feels alive

Mix clear speech, court sounds and licensed music to suit each platform.

Final creative check

Clear first-frame hook; complete subtitles; accurate copy; correct logo; consistent type and colour; considered pacing; sound clearance; working call to action; final platform export.

07 / Paid media & acquisition

Paid campaigns with clear measures.

Meta campaigns provide the initial paid approach. TikTok advertising can be tested where organic results and conversion tracking justify it.

Stage 01 · Before bookings

Awareness & interest

Reach relevant nearby audiences with the club experience, location and beginner-friendly proposition.

Measure: local reach, video engagement, registrations
Stage 02 · Booking release

Action & bookings

Use strong offers and friction-free landing journeys to convert engaged prospects into first sessions.

Measure: booking starts, purchases, CPA
Stage 03 · Established operation

Retention & repeat play

Promote appropriate sessions, community events and repeat booking opportunities to qualified audiences.

Measure: repeat bookings, cost, attributable value

Audience & creative testing

  • Nearby sport and leisure audiences, local social groups and relevant newcomers.
  • Test brand, beginner, competitive and social hooks with distinct creative variants.
  • Use geo-relevant messaging; avoid over-narrow targeting before evidence exists.

Campaign management

  • Account and campaign structure, naming conventions and placement-specific assets.
  • Creative testing, audience monitoring, budget pacing and exclusions as appropriate.
  • Landing-page checks, event verification, UTM discipline and weekly optimisation.
A provisional £3,000 media reserve is available for phased activation, subject to an agreed budget and live, measurable registration or booking actions. Media spend is separate from management fees.
